# Weather statistics

1. Turn relative dates into exact dates. "Last year" means the previous calendar year.
2. Call `weather_stat` for one period, or `yearly_history` for change across years.
3. "Had snowfall" uses the tool's default threshold. State the threshold and the dates in your answer.
4. Say the data is modelled reanalysis, so it can differ from local station reports.
5. A trend is a simple descriptive slope. Don't call it statistically significant or attribute a cause.
6. Don't describe a partial year as a full year.
